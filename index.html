03 / Our approach

An engineering mindset.
A practical purpose.

Founded and developed by Nguyen Xuan Thanh, a mechanical engineer based in Hanoi, Vietnam. For enquiries and pilot discussions: xuanthanh@umefor.space.

Umefor is an early-stage, independent software initiative shaped by a mechanical engineer’s approach to problem-solving: understand the task, define the constraints, build a small working system, and test it.

We explore how AI can help people interpret information and move through complex work, while keeping validation, data ownership and important decisions in human hands.

  • 01
    Start with a specific workflow, not a universal platform.
  • 02
    Keep model suggestions separate from verified results.
  • 03
    Make progress inspectable and limitations visible.
